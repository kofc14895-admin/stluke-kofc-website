What we do

Faith in action, close to home.

Our work falls into four areas: Faith, Family, Community and Life. Here is what the council is doing.

Faith

Faith

Growing together in prayer and in the life of St. Luke.

Knights Masses and prayerto confirm

Special liturgies, prayer, and welcome ceremonies for new Knights.

Parish Masses

We usher, read, and serve as Eucharistic ministers at fifth Sunday Masses.

Vocationsto confirm

Support for seminarian education, and for Blessed Sacrament Monastery.

Family

Family

Good food, good company, and something for every age.

Breakfasts and fish fries

Pancake breakfasts, Lenten fish fries, and our food booth at the St. Luke Fall Festival. They bring the parish together and raise money for charity.

Youth and family activitiesto confirm

The spring Bike Rodeo, the Free Throw Contest and the Soccer Kick, plus cookouts and bocce for Knights and their families.

Parish celebrations

We pitch in with the MOYC St. Patrick’s Day party, SMRE and MOYC breakfasts, and the Mary’s Way dinner.

Community

Community

Looking out for our neighbors, near and far.

Coats for Kids

Each year we provide winter coats for children at Holy Angels School.

Special Olympics Indiana

Financial support, help preparing for competitions, and Knights serving as officials at events.

Gibault Children’s Services

Founded by Indiana Knights in 1921. We support Gibault financially and encourage members to volunteer.

40 Cans for Lent

A Lenten food drive for the Boulevard Place Food Pantry, with a goal of 10,000 pounds.

Blood drives

Regular drives with the Indiana Blood Bank.

Life

Life

Honoring the dignity of every life, from the womb to natural death.

Baby Bottle Driveto confirm

Parishioners fill baby bottles with change to support mothers and babies.

Prayer and witnessto confirm

40 Days for Life vigils and the Life Chain.

Support for mothers and babies

Gifts to the Women’s Care Center, Birthline, and Right to Life.

Want to be part of it?

Every one of these runs on volunteer Knights. If you’re curious, come to an event and say hello.
