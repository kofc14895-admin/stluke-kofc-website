Who we are

We are parishioners of St. Luke, Catholic men who serve God, country and family through the Knights of Columbus. Our four principles are charity, unity, fraternity and patriotism.

What we do

Faith

Growing together in faith. Knights Masses, prayer, and welcome ceremonies.

Family

Bringing families together. Pancake breakfasts, Lenten fish fries, and Fall Fest.

Life

Honoring the dignity of every life. Baby Bottle Drive, 40 Days for Life, and support for families.

Community

Serving our neighbors. Blood drives, Coats for Kids, and the food pantry.
