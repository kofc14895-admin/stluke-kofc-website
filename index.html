Knights in Action

Faith, Family, Life, and Community: the ways we put our faith into action at St. Luke.

Faith

Growing together in faith. Knights Masses, special liturgies, prayer, welcome ceremonies, and parish traditions.

Family

Bringing families together. Pancake breakfasts, Lenten fish fries, Fall Fest, youth sports, and cookouts.

♡Baby Bottle Drive

Life

Honoring the dignity of every life. Baby Bottle Drive, 40 Days for Life, and support for mothers, babies, and families.

Community

Serving our neighbors. Blood drives, Coats for Kids, 40 Cans for Lent, food pantry support, and outreach.

Your generosity at work

When our parish comes together, good things happen.

Every breakfast, fish fry, and fundraiser helps us support people and causes that matter.

  • Children: Coats for Kids, Special Olympics Indiana, Gibault Children’s Services
  • Mothers and babies: Women’s Care Center and Birthline
  • Neighbors: Boulevard Place Food Pantry
  • Future priests: Seminarian education
  • The wider Church: Christian Refugee Relief Fund

Thank you, St. Luke.

Your participation and generosity help turn simple parish gatherings into meaningful support for our neighbors.
