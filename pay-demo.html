Online payments

Pay dues, enter an event, or give.

Payments are processed securely by Square, and you receive a receipt by email. You don’t need a Square account.

  1. 1
    Choose what to pay for
    Pick an item below and add it to your cart.
  2. 2
    Add your name
    For dues, also your member number, so we credit the right account.
  3. 3
    Check out
    Enter your email for the receipt. Bring it, or just give your name, at the event.
