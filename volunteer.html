Put your faith into action.

Our events run on volunteer Knights. If you’re a member, this is where you find out how to jump in.

Ways Knights help

  • Cook and serve at fish fries, pancake breakfasts and the Fall Festival.
  • Collect and deliver for 40 Cans for Lent and other drives.
  • Set up and clean up at council and parish events.
  • Help with blood drives and Special Olympics events.
  • Lend a skill. Photography, accounting, tech, carpentry: we can use it.

Members: sign up and log your hours
